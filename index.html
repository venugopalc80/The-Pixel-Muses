What does The Pixel Muses do?

The Pixel Muses is a UK-based website development and digital solutions company. We build, redesign and improve business websites, online stores and digital platforms, with SEO, local search, digital growth, software and AI automation available as connected services.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the scope, content, integrations and level of customisation. We scope the project first and provide a clear quote before work starts.

How long does a website take?

Smaller projects can move quickly; larger builds take longer. After discovery, we'll give you a realistic delivery plan rather than an arbitrary promise.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. A redesign isn't always the right answer. We can audit and improve your existing site, search visibility, conversion, performance and surrounding business profiles.

Do you provide SEO?

Yes. Our search work can include technical SEO, local SEO, structured data, content architecture, business profiles, AEO and GEO.

Do you build custom software?

Yes. We build web applications, dashboards, portals, APIs, internal tools, automation and custom digital platforms.

Can you add AI to an existing business?

Yes, where there is a useful problem to solve. We can design AI-assisted workflows, integrations and product features around measurable outcomes.

Do I own my website?

Project ownership and access are agreed clearly in the proposal. We aim to leave clients with a maintainable digital asset, not a locked box.

Do you work outside the UK?

Yes. We work with businesses across the UK, USA, India and remotely.
