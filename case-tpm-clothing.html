TPM Clothing — making the collection feel considered online.

An editorial commerce concept balancing brand presentation, product discovery and a simple path to purchase.

PROJECTTPM Clothing
TYPEConcept / Portfolio Build
FOCUSE-commerce UX

THE BRIEF

Put the brand before the catalogue.

The concept uses editorial spacing, product-led imagery and a restrained interface so the clothing remains the visual focus.

Navigation and shopping paths are kept deliberately simple: discover the collection, understand the product and move towards purchase without unnecessary friction.
